<objective>
Give the doctor the ability to take an entry off their schedule, with the one rule that matters enforced on the server: an entry a patient has already booked can never be removed this way. Deleting a blocked row is how a doctor un-blocks a period, so this one endpoint serves both AVAIL-04 and the un-block half of AVAIL-06.

Purpose: this is the destructive half of schedule control and the place where a careless implementation orphans a patient's appointment. The status check must be app logic reading live database state, because RLS proves ownership and cannot express "deletable only while not booked".

Output: `DELETE /api/doctor/slots/[id]`, the per-row delete control and its two confirm dialogs on `/doctor/schedule`, and the delete e2e spec.

Scope guard: deleting a blocked period removes the restriction and nothing else — no compensating insert, ever (D-02). There is no un-block endpoint and no status-toggle route (D-01).
</objective>

| browser -> `DELETE /api/doctor/slots/[id]` | An arbitrary uuid crosses here, chosen entirely by the caller |
