<objective>
Add the four structured filters — specialty, spoken language, neighborhood and availability date range — on top of the proven name-search path, all combining with AND and all persisted in the URL.

Purpose: SEARCH-02 through SEARCH-06 are the substance of "find a doctor matching my criteria". Three of the four are one extra predicate on the view. The fourth, availability, is deliberately NOT the same computation as the sort key: "has at least one slot in this range" and "what is the earliest slot overall" are different questions, and conflating them silently hides doctors who do have a matching slot later in their schedule.

Output: the filter panel component, the extended validation module, the AND-combined query with its availability pre-query, and the filter coverage in the search spec.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signal:** pluralization — "combining multiple filters in a single search" (detector: `assumption-delta scan 3`, `detected: true`).

**Noun now primary:** the *filter set* (a collection of independent, individually optional predicates), not a single filter.

**Decision:** `no-change`.

**Rationale:** the identity model was already plural from the outset. `ParsedSearchParams` is a record of independently nullable fields, the route applies each predicate conditionally and relies on Supabase-JS implicit AND chaining (D-09), and the URL carries one query parameter per filter (D-13). Nothing in the codebase models "the filter" as a singular entity that would need promoting to a collection — there is no single active-filter slot, no filter id, and no last-filter-wins behaviour to migrate. Adding a filter in a future phase is one more nullable field plus one more conditional predicate, with no shape change. Advisory only; no plan task changes as a result.
